nkt-train — Manual Seat Availability Helper

This page helps non-technical users collect seat-availability from IRCTC (or other sources) and create a shareable summary link. No APIs, no scraping — you will manually paste the availability text from the source.

How to get availability (hand-holding):
  1. Open IRCTC website or app and search your train + date.
  2. Locate the coach availability section — it usually shows statuses like AVAILABLE, RAC, WL, or NOT AVAILABLE with numbers.
  3. Select and copy the text (or type it manually). Example copy: "SL: WL 12, 3A: RAC 2, 2A: AVAILABLE 10".
  4. Paste that text into the box above and click "Parse & Share".
If you only have a screenshot, you can transcribe the small availability lines into the box — keep it simple.
